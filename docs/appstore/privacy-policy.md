# lungful privacy policy

*publish this page at halfday.dev (e.g. /lungful/privacy) before submission — App Store Connect requires a live privacy policy URL.*

---

**effective date: [set on publish]**

lungful collects no data. this page is short because there is nothing to disclose.

**what lungful stores.** your saved breathing patterns and app preferences are stored on your device, in the app's local storage. they never leave it. lungful has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and makes no network connections.

**what we can see about you.** nothing. we cannot see your patterns, your usage, or that you use lungful at all. purchase records are handled entirely by Apple under their privacy policy.

**third parties.** none. lungful contains no third-party code, SDKs, or trackers.

**children.** lungful collects no data from anyone, including children.

**changes.** if a future version of lungful ever changes any of the above, this policy and the app's App Store privacy label will be updated first, before the change ships.

**contact.** questions: via halfday.dev.
